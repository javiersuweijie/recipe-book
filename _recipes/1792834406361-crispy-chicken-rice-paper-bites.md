---
layout: recipe
title: Crispy Chicken Rice Paper Bites
description: Ground chicken mixed with ginger, scallion, and aromatic spices, wrapped in rice paper and air-fried until shatteringly crispy. Serve with a spicy dipping sauce for an irresistible snack or appetizer.
servings: 4 (10-12 bites)
prep_time: 15 minutes
cook_time: 12 minutes
ingredients:
  - 250g (½ lb) minced chicken
  - 2 tablespoons (30ml) soy sauce
  - 1 tablespoon (15ml) red chilli sauce
  - 1 tablespoon (15ml) chilli oil
  - 2 spring onions, finely chopped
  - 2 tablespoons onion, finely chopped
  - 1 tablespoon fresh ginger, grated
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - ½ teaspoon salt
  - ½ teaspoon black pepper
  - ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  - ½ teaspoon cumin powder
  - "For Wrapping":
    - 5-6 rice paper sheets (round)
    - Water for soaking
  - "Spicy Dipping Sauce":
    - 2 tablespoons (30ml) soy sauce
    - 1 tablespoon (15ml) rice vinegar
    - 1 teaspoon chilli oil
    - 1 teaspoon sesame oil
    - 1 teaspoon sugar
    - 1 clove garlic, minced
notes:
  - Make sure to cut the rolled parcels into bite-sized pieces before air frying so the rice paper crisps up evenly
  - Work quickly when handling the rice paper - it becomes more fragile the longer it sits after soaking
  - For extra crunch, lightly brush or spray the bites with oil before air frying
  - Serve immediately for maximum crispiness
  - Recipe adapted from Every Last Bite / Carmen Sturdy
---

1. In a large bowl, combine the minced chicken, soy sauce, red chilli sauce, chilli oil, spring onions, onion, grated ginger, garlic, salt, black pepper, red chilli powder, and cumin powder. Mix well until everything is evenly incorporated.

2. Fill a shallow dish or wide bowl with room-temperature water. Dip one rice paper sheet at a time into the water for about 10-15 seconds until just softened and pliable. Lay it flat on a clean work surface.

3. Place about 2 tablespoons of the chicken filling along one edge of the softened rice paper. Fold the sides in, then roll tightly into a spring-roll shaped parcel. Repeat with remaining rice paper and filling.

4. Using a sharp knife, cut each rolled parcel into bite-sized pieces (about 3-4 pieces per roll).

5. Preheat your air fryer to 180°C (350°F). Arrange the bites in a single layer in the air fryer basket, making sure they don't touch each other.

6. Air fry for 10-12 minutes, flipping halfway through, until the rice paper is golden, bubbled, and shatteringly crispy, and the chicken is cooked through.

7. Meanwhile, prepare the dipping sauce: whisk together soy sauce, rice vinegar, chilli oil, sesame oil, sugar, and minced garlic in a small bowl.

8. Serve the crispy chicken rice paper bites immediately with the spicy dipping sauce on the side.
