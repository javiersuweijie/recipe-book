---
layout: recipe
title: Sticky Garlic-Honey Pork Belly
description: A savoury-sweet pork belly dish with a glossy garlic-honey glaze — quick, sticky, and irresistible served with steamed rice
servings: 2–3
prep_time: 5 minutes
cook_time: 10 minutes
ingredients:
  - "Pork Belly":
    - 400–450 g pork belly, cut into bite-size pieces
  - "Sauce":
    - 2 tbsp soy sauce
    - 2 tbsp rice wine
    - 1 tbsp oyster sauce
    - 1 tsp dark vinegar
  - "Aromatics & Finishing":
    - 10 g garlic, minced
    - 10 g unsalted butter
    - 1 tbsp honey
    - Chopped coriander/cilantro, to taste
    - A little cooking oil
notes:
  - The sauce reduces quickly toward the end — keep the pork moving to avoid burning
  - Serve immediately while the glaze is still glossy; it firms up as it cools
  - This dish pairs beautifully with plain steamed rice or jasmine rice
---

1. **Mix the sauce.** Combine the soy sauce, rice wine, oyster sauce, and dark vinegar in a small bowl.

2. **Brown the pork belly.** Heat a small amount of oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the pork belly in a single layer. Cook until browned, then turn the pieces and brown the other side, about 5–6 minutes total.

3. **Add the sauce.** Pour the prepared sauce over the pork belly and bring it to a boil.

4. **Add the garlic.** Stir in the minced garlic and cook until fragrant.

5. **Add butter and honey.** Add the butter and honey. Stir everything together and continue cooking over medium to medium-low heat.

6. **Reduce until sticky.** Let the sauce bubble and reduce, stirring and tossing the pork frequently. It's ready when the sauce becomes thick, glossy, and coats the pork belly.

7. **Finish.** Transfer to a plate and top generously with chopped coriander/cilantro. Serve immediately.
